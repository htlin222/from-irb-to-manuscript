## Editorial office (format)

E1. The structured abstract must not exceed 250 words and must use the headings Background, Materials and Methods, Results, Conclusion.

E2. In-text citations must be Arabic numerals in superscript square brackets placed after punctuation, and the reference list must follow the journal (Vancouver/ICMJE) style.

E3. Figure legends must be 40 words or fewer. Please also confirm the Data Availability Statement and the disclosure of any generative AI use required by the journal.

## Reviewer 1

R1.1. Treatment changed with calendar time. Dual blockade was used mostly in the later years and single blockade mostly in the earlier years. Please explain how the analysis separates the effect of the regimen from the effect of the era, and show whether the two groups truly overlap (for example, propensity score distributions by group).

R1.2. Adjuvant T-DM1 for residual disease became available during the later part of the study period. Because this was given mainly in the dual-blockade era, it may account for part of the EFS difference. Please address this with a sensitivity analysis.

R1.3. Patients who progressed before surgery or had surgery elsewhere have no pathologic response. Please state how they were handled in the pCR analysis and whether the conclusion changes under alternative handling.

R1.4. Residual confounding is likely in a retrospective cohort. Please quantify how strong an unmeasured confounder would need to be to explain away the main findings.

## Reviewer 2

R2.1. Please define time zero precisely and explain how the design avoids immortal time bias.

R2.2. Follow-up is shorter for patients treated in recent years. Please report median follow-up by group (reverse Kaplan–Meier) and consider a restricted time horizon for the survival comparisons.

R2.3. The overall survival analysis has few events. The conclusion regarding OS should be worded cautiously, with the confidence interval emphasized rather than the P value.

R2.4. The Discussion should compare the findings with NeoSphere, TRYPHAENA, and published real-world cohorts, and should list the limitations of a single-center design.
