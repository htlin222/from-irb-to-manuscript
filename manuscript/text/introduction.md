Adding pertuzumab to neoadjuvant trastuzumab and chemotherapy increased the rate of pathologic complete response (pCR) in randomized trials of human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-positive breast cancer.[@gianni2012neosphere; @shao2020peony] Patients who achieve pCR have longer event-free survival,[@cortazar2014ctneobc] but the long-term benefit of pertuzumab is modest.[@loibl2024aphinityos] Observational studies have reported higher pCR rates with dual HER2 blockade,[@matsas2026metaanalysis] yet the two regimens were often used in different years, which complicates comparison. We compared neoadjuvant trastuzumab plus pertuzumab with trastuzumab alone in routine practice, using methods designed for limited overlap between treatment groups.
