## Data Availability Statement

The datasets generated and/or analyzed during the current study are not publicly available due to patient privacy regulations but are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request. In this teaching demonstration, all data are synthetic.

## Use of Generative AI

This demonstration manuscript, including all of its text, was written by an AI model (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic) at the request of the authors for teaching purposes; in a real submission, the text must be written by the authors, as required by the journal's policy on generative AI. Claude Opus 5.5 was also used, under the authors' direction, to write and test the statistical analysis code, check the data, produce the tables and figures, and format the manuscript. OpenEvidence was used to search the literature, and every reference was verified against Crossref and PubMed. The analysis plan was specified by the authors before outcomes were examined and was approved by the institutional review board. The authors are responsible for reviewing all code, results, and text and take full responsibility for the content. The analysis code can be provided as supplementary material.
